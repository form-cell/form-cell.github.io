About the Startup

Engineering innovation and intellectual property driven product development for practical technologies.

Square-Cut Research and Innovation Private Limited is focused on engineering innovation and intellectual property driven product development. It works on practical technologies aimed at solving real-world engineering challenges in sports infrastructure, cyber laws, cyber security and other engineering domains.

Founder: ILU KUMARI.

The startup is located at Bhagalpur, Bihar. It has been recognized under Startup India with DIPP recognition number DIPP135531 and has received support under Startup Bihar.

Its primary objective is to develop scalable engineering technologies backed by strong intellectual property.
